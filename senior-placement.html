Senior Placement in Texas

Mercy Senior Solutions provides senior placement services across Texas, with local senior advisors who know the assisted living and memory care landscape in the communities we serve. That familiarity means we can quickly narrow down options that fit a family's budget, licensing requirements, and location preferences, rather than starting from a generic national list.

Because our advisors work directly with Texas communities day to day, they understand local regulations, typical wait times, and which providers are the right fit for specific care needs — so families get guidance that's grounded in the market they're actually searching in.

Memory Care Placement

Memory care placement involves more than finding an open bed — it means evaluating how a community is actually equipped to support residents living with Alzheimer's or other forms of dementia. We look closely at staff-to-resident ratios, secured environments, staff training in dementia-specific care techniques, and the structure of daily programming before recommending a community to a family.

When we evaluate a memory care community, we ask the same questions a family would want answered: how staff de-escalate moments of confusion or agitation, how medication management is handled, and how the physical layout is designed to reduce wandering risk while still feeling like home. Families considering memory care should look for consistent staffing, clear communication practices, and a community that welcomes ongoing family involvement.

Assisted Living Placement

Our assisted living placement process starts with a full assessment of a senior's care needs, mobility, budget, and personal preferences. From there, our advisors match those needs against our network of vetted communities, narrowing the list to a handful of realistic options rather than overwhelming families with every facility in the area.

Once a shortlist is ready, we help coordinate community tours so families can see the environment firsthand and ask questions directly of staff. After a decision is made, our advisors stay involved through move-in coordination, helping with paperwork and logistics so the transition is as smooth as possible.
